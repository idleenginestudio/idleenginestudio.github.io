影子保險箱 隱私權政策

Shadow Vault Privacy Policy · 生效日期 Effective 2026-10-29

中文English

Idle Engine Studio（以下稱「我們」）開發的《影子保險箱》有 Android App 和網頁版。這份政策說明各版本會處理哪些資料。

我們自己不收集個人資料

遊戲不需要註冊或登入。關卡進度、星星、寶石、道具（燈泡、愛心）和設定只存在你的裝置（瀏覽器或 App 的本機儲存空間）裡，不會傳到我們的伺服器。刪除 App 或清除瀏覽器資料就會一起刪除。

寶石

Android 版有遊戲內貨幣「寶石」，只能靠過關或看獎勵廣告取得，用來換提示燈泡和愛心。寶石不能用真錢購買，也沒有任何內購；寶石只記錄在你的裝置上，沒有現金價值，不能轉讓或兌換。

網頁版

網頁版放在 itch.io 和工作室網站（GitHub Pages），沒有廣告，也沒有寶石，我們不收集資料。網站或平台為了提供服務可能會記錄 IP 位址等連線資訊，請參考 itch.io 與 GitHub 的隱私權政策。

廣告（Google AdMob）

這個 App 用 Google AdMob 顯示獎勵廣告（由你選擇觀看：提示燈泡用完時多拿一顆、愛心用完時復活、無盡模式接關、結算時寶石加倍）和插頁廣告（離開結算畫面時偶爾出現，最初幾局不會出現，之後最多每 3 局一次）。為了顯示廣告、計算成效、限制同一則廣告出現的次數和防止詐騙，Google 可能會收集並使用：

  • 裝置的廣告 ID（Android 廣告 ID）
  • IP 位址，以及由 IP 推算的大略位置（國家、城市）
  • 裝置和系統資訊（機型、作業系統版本、語言）
  • 你和廣告的互動（看了哪則廣告、有沒有點擊）以及當機紀錄

在歐洲經濟區、英國和瑞士，第一次開啟 App 時會先看到 Google 的同意視窗，你可以選擇是否同意 Google 為個人化廣告使用你的資料；不同意時仍可能看到非個人化廣告。想重新選擇，可以在 Android 的「設定 → 應用程式」清除這個 App 的資料，下次開啟時會再出現同意視窗。

你也可以在 Android 的「設定 → Google → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID，或到 Google 我的廣告中心關閉個人化廣告。Google 怎麼使用這些資料：Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料、Google 隱私權政策。

資料分享

我們不會出售你的資料。除了上面提到的 Google 服務，我們不會把資料提供給其他人，除非法律要求。

兒童

這個 App 不是以 13 歲以下兒童為主要對象，我們也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。如果你認為孩子提供了個人資料給我們，請聯絡我們，我們會刪除。

你的權利

你可以要求查看、更正或刪除與你有關的資料，也可以撤回同意。請透過下面的聯絡方式告訴我們。Google 收集的廣告資料，請用上面 Google 提供的設定管理。

政策變更

如果這份政策有變更，我們會更新這一頁和上面的日期。

English

Shadow Vault by Idle Engine Studio ("we") comes as an Android app and a web version. This policy explains what data each one handles.

We collect no personal data ourselves

There is no sign-up or login. Progress, stars, gems, items (bulbs and hearts) and settings are stored only on your device (the browser's or the app's local storage) and never sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes them.

Gems

The Android app has an in-game currency, gems, earned only by clearing vaults or watching rewarded ads and spent on hint bulbs and hearts. Gems cannot be bought with real money and there are no in-app purchases; they are stored only on your device, have no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed.

Web version

The web version is hosted on itch.io and the studio site (GitHub Pages). It has no ads and no gems, and we collect no data. The site or portal may log connection information such as your IP address to provide the service; see the itch.io and GitHub privacy policies.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads (which you choose to watch: an extra hint bulb when you run out, a revive when your hearts run out, a continue in endless mode, or double gems at the results) and interstitial ads (occasionally when you leave the results screen — never in the first few runs, and at most every 3rd run after that) through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, cap how often an ad appears and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and system information, your interactions with ads, and crash data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you first see Google's consent form and choose whether Google may use your data for personalised ads; without consent you may still see non-personalised ads. To choose again, clear the app's data in Android Settings → Apps and the form appears on the next launch. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or turn off personalised ads in My Ad Center. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Apart from the Google services above, we do not share data with anyone unless the law requires it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete data about you, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. Advertising data collected by Google is managed through Google's settings linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
